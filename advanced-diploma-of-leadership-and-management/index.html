A practical guide to the Advanced Diploma of Leadership and Management (BSB60420)

BSB60420 — the Advanced Diploma of Leadership and Management — is a nationally recognised AQF Level 6 qualification. On the Advanced Diploma of Leadership and Management course page, 3CIR Online sets out a 100% online, self-paced pathway. Students complete 10 units: 5 core and 5 elective. The published unit list includes applying critical thinking for complex problem solving, leading and managing organisational change, providing leadership across the organisation, developing and implementing business plans, managing innovation and continuous improvement and communicating with influence. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualification.

The published full price is $4,450, GST-free. Entry is limited to people who have completed a Diploma or Advanced Diploma from the BSB Training Package (current or a superseded equivalent version), or who have two years of equivalent full-time relevant workplace experience in an operational or leadership role. Intermediate language, literacy, numeracy and digital skills are also required. Study sits inside a 24-month enrolment term, and the AQF Volume of Learning listed on the course page is 1,800–2,400 hours. Assessment is practical rather than exams, and an allocated trainer marks submissions. Roles listed on the page include Project Manager, Health Services Manager, Human Resources Manager, Operations Manager, Sales & Marketing Manager and Business / Management Consultant.

People who already use these skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning rather than studying the units as a course. 3CIR offers Recognition of Prior Learning for Advanced Diploma of Leadership and Management at $2,150, fixed, all-inclusive and GST-free. The first assessment is free and without obligation. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course, and the RPL price is the full fixed price.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. Current course details, unit lists and enrolment steps are on the course page.
