The four units on a BSBSS00112 Statement of Attainment

The Workplace Technology Skill Set is a nationally recognised skill set, code BSBSS00112, of four basic technology units. Completing it through 3CIR Online leads to a Statement of Attainment, not a full qualification. The published BSBSS00112 page is explicit about that outcome.

The statement records Research using the internet; Design and produce business documents; Design and produce spreadsheets; and Create electronic presentations. That is a basic workplace-technology cluster, and it is not the same award as the Digital Business Administration Skill Set, which deals with complex documents, digital solutions and online collaboration.

The program is online and self-paced, with no exams. Asset College issues the Statement of Attainment, as named on the course page.

On enrolment, the course page sets out a short list of conditions. training.gov.au lists no entry requirements for the skill set. A Unique Student Identifier, photo identification, and an LLND check (or a Certificate III or higher) are still required, and places are for domestic students. Eligibility is limited to Australian citizens, permanent residents, and New Zealand citizens who live in Australia. People overseas or on a student visa cannot enrol, and the offering is not CRICOS-registered.

Read the unit list on the Workplace Technology Skill Set page at 3CIR Online before you enrol.

A Statement of Attainment for BSBSS00112 is only as useful as the unit list it records. For the Workplace Technology Skill Set, that list is the cluster around internet research, business documents, spreadsheets and electronic presentations. The Workplace Technology Skill Set keeps the unit titles, the statement-of-attainment wording, and the enrolment conditions in one place, which is the safest way to check the scope before you apply.
