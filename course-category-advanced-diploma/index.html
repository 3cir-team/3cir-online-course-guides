A guide to Advanced Diploma courses online with 3CIR Online

Two nationally recognised Advanced Diploma qualifications are listed on the nationally recognised advanced Diploma courses online page. They sit at AQF Level 6. The list is the BSB60120 Advanced Diploma of Business and the BSB60420 Advanced Diploma of Leadership and Management. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualifications. 3CIR Online delivers the courses 100% online and self-paced.

The category page places both credentials at the senior manager and director-track tier of the vocational pathway. The Advanced Diploma of Business is described as covering strategic planning, executive-level finance, organisational governance and cross-functional leadership, for managers moving toward senior manager, department head and director-track roles. The Advanced Diploma of Leadership and Management is described as covering organisational strategy, executive decision-making, multi-team leadership, performance frameworks and change management, as a pathway toward operations director, regional manager and general-manager-track roles. Each qualification comprises 10 units (five core and five elective) and is studied inside a 24-month enrolment term. Entry is limited: you need a Diploma or Advanced Diploma from the BSB Training Package (current or a superseded equivalent version), or two years of equivalent full-time relevant workplace experience in an operational or leadership role, plus intermediate language, literacy, numeracy and digital skills.

The page points to destinations such as Senior Business Manager, Operations Director, Business Unit Manager, HR Director, Regional Manager and Strategy Manager. The page describes an Advanced Diploma as a bachelor-equivalent vocational credential. Assessment is practical rather than exams, and an allocated trainer marks submissions.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. People who already use the relevant skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning instead of studying the units as a course. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course. Current unit lists, entry rules and enrolment steps are on each individual course page, and on the category page itself.
