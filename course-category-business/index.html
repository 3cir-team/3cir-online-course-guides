A guide to Online Business courses with 3CIR Online

Seven nationally recognised business qualifications are listed on the Online Business courses page. They sit at from Certificate II through to Advanced Diploma. The list is the BSB20120 Certificate II in Workplace Skills, the BSB30120 Certificate III in Business, the BSB30220 Certificate III in Entrepreneurship and New Business, the BSB40120 Certificate IV in Business, the BSB40320 Certificate IV in Entrepreneurship and New Business, the BSB50120 Diploma of Business and the BSB60120 Advanced Diploma of Business. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualifications. 3CIR Online delivers the courses 100% online and self-paced.

The landing page presents business study as a versatile pathway that can start in administration, move into coordination or management, or support someone launching a venture. It notes that business-qualified staff are needed across operations, project coordination, account management, administration and leadership, so the same family of qualifications can apply in many industries. Study is 100% online and self-paced, with practical assessment rather than exams and feedback from an allocated trainer. Some higher-level qualifications in the group have formal entry rules; the Advanced Diploma, for example, requires a prior BSB Diploma or Advanced Diploma, or two years of equivalent relevant experience.

The page points to destinations such as Office Manager, Operations Coordinator, Account Manager, Operations Manager, Small Business Owner and General Manager. If you are choosing a starting point, the Certificate II and Certificate III pages are the usual first step; Certificate IV and Diploma pages are aimed at people coordinating or leading work.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. People who already use the relevant skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning instead of studying the units as a course. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course. Current unit lists, entry rules and enrolment steps are on each individual course page, and on the category page itself.
