What a Statement of Attainment covers for BSBSS00128

The Lead Auditor Skill Set is a nationally recognised four-unit skill set, code BSBSS00128. The outcome through 3CIR Online is a Statement of Attainment, not a Diploma of Quality Auditing. That is stated on the published BSBSS00128 page.

The statement records Participate in quality audits; Initiate quality audits; Lead quality audits; and Report on quality audits. Those titles follow a complete audit from participation through to the report. They are the whole of this award.

Assessment is by practical workplace tasks rather than exams, and study is self-paced. Asset College issues the Statement of Attainment under the third-party arrangement described below.

On enrolment, the course page sets out a short list of conditions. training.gov.au lists no entry requirements for the skill set. A Unique Student Identifier, photo identification, and an LLND check (or a Certificate III or higher) are still required, and places are for domestic students. Eligibility is limited to Australian citizens, permanent residents, and New Zealand citizens who live in Australia. People overseas or on a student visa cannot enrol, and the offering is not CRICOS-registered.

Confirm the unit titles and the assessment note on the Lead Auditor Skill Set page at 3CIR Online.

A Statement of Attainment for BSBSS00128 is only as useful as the unit list it records. For the Lead Auditor Skill Set, that list is the cluster around participating in, initiating, leading and reporting on quality audits. The Lead Auditor Skill Set keeps the unit titles, the statement-of-attainment wording, and the enrolment conditions in one place, which is the safest way to check the scope before you apply.
