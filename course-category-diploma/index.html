A guide to Diploma courses online with 3CIR Online

Eight nationally recognised Diploma qualifications are listed on the nationally recognised diploma courses online page. They sit at AQF Level 5. The list is the BSB50120 Diploma of Business, the BSB50420 Diploma of Leadership and Management, the BSB50820 Diploma of Project Management, the BSB50920 Diploma of Quality Auditing, the BSB51319 Diploma of Work Health and Safety, the CPP50619 Diploma of Security Risk Management, the PSP50316 Diploma of Government Security and the PSP50416 Diploma of Government Investigations. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualifications. 3CIR Online delivers the courses 100% online and self-paced.

The landing page frames these Diplomas as manager-level credentials for people who already coordinate work and want a nationally recognised qualification in a chosen field. Study is 100% online and self-paced, typically inside an 18-month enrolment window, with practical assessment rather than exams and an allocated trainer. Not every Diploma has the same entry rule. The Diploma of Work Health and Safety requires completion of the core units of BSB41419 Certificate IV in Work Health and Safety, or mapped-equivalent predecessor competencies. The Diploma of Security Risk Management requires one of CPP40707 Certificate IV in Security and Risk Management, CPP40719 Certificate IV in Security Management, or CPP41519 Certificate IV in Security Risk Analysis. The other Diplomas on the page should still be checked individually for any stated prerequisites.

The page points to destinations such as roles in business management, leadership, project management, quality auditing, work health and safety, security risk management and government investigations, depending on the qualification you choose. Use the category page to compare the eight options, then open the course page for the exact unit mix and elective rules.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. People who already use the relevant skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning instead of studying the units as a course. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course. Current unit lists, entry rules and enrolment steps are on each individual course page, and on the category page itself.
