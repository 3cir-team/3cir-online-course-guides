The three units recorded for PSPSS00040

The Government Investigations Skill Set is a nationally recognised skill set, code PSPSS00040, of three units only. Completing it through 3CIR Online leads to a Statement of Attainment, not a full government investigations qualification. The published PSPSS00040 page states that it is not a full qualification.

The statement records Plan and initiate an investigation; Conduct an investigation; and Finalise an investigation. Those three titles cover the life of a regulatory investigation and nothing beyond that cluster. People who need a longer government investigations qualification should not treat this skill set as a substitute.

Delivery is online and self-paced, with no exams. Asset College issues the Statement of Attainment, as named on the course page.

On enrolment, the course page sets out a short list of conditions. training.gov.au lists no entry requirements for the skill set. A Unique Student Identifier, photo identification, and an LLND check (or a Certificate III or higher) are still required, and places are for domestic students. Eligibility is limited to Australian citizens, permanent residents, and New Zealand citizens who live in Australia. People overseas or on a student visa cannot enrol, and the offering is not CRICOS-registered.

Read the three unit titles on the Government Investigations Skill Set page at 3CIR Online.

A Statement of Attainment for PSPSS00040 is only as useful as the unit list it records. For the Government Investigations Skill Set, that list is the cluster around planning, conducting and finalising a regulatory investigation. The Government Investigations Skill Set keeps the unit titles, the statement-of-attainment wording, and the enrolment conditions in one place, which is the safest way to check the scope before you apply.
