Recognition of prior learning for people in Western Australia

Western Australia is included in 3CIR's Australia-wide recognition of prior learning service. People in Perth and in regional Western Australia deal with the Brisbane-based team by phone, email and online, rather than through a local branch.

The state page, RPL for people in Western Australia, describes that pathway. Qualifications under discussion may range from Certificate II to Graduate Diploma, depending on a person's evidence and the award selected.

This short guide does not replace the live Western Australia page and does not promise a result. It exists so that readers in the state can find the public outline and the contact details in one place.

You can reach 3CIR on 1300 517 039 or at info@3cir.com. The registered office is Suite 676, 190 Oxford St, Bulimba QLD 4171. Hours are Monday to Friday, 8:30am–4:00pm (AEST).

3CIR is not a registered training organisation. Qualifications are issued by Asset College (RTO 31718) under a third-party arrangement with 3CIR.
