A short guide to Certificate III recognition of prior learning

People who have already built skills on the job sometimes ask whether those skills can count toward a Certificate III. In the Australian system, recognition of prior learning is the formal assessment that compares a person's evidence with the units in a chosen qualification.

3CIR publishes a public page on this topic so that conversation can start from a clear outline rather than from rumour. Read Certificate III recognition of prior learning with 3CIR for the current explanation of Certificate III RPL, including how evidence is typically used and what the assessment is — and is not.

The page is written for people in Australia. It does not treat RPL as a shortcut around the standard, and it does not describe the process as a taught course from the beginning. It is an introduction to an assessment pathway, and the live guide is the place to read the detail.
