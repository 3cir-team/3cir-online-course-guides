What BSBSS00127 records on a Statement of Attainment

The Contact Centre Team Manager Skill Set is a nationally recognised skill set, not a full qualification. Its code is BSBSS00127. When the four units are completed through 3CIR Online, Asset College issues a Statement of Attainment listing BSBSS00127 and those units. The published BSBSS00127 page states that the award is not a full qualification.

The four units are Lead difficult conversations; Implement customer service strategies; Manage team effectiveness; and Create a customer-centric culture. They are written for people managing a team in a customer service contact centre. The statement does not confer a leadership diploma or certificate.

3CIR Online delivers the skill set online and self-paced, with no exams. The issuing body remains Asset College, as named on the course page.

On enrolment, the course page sets out a short list of conditions. training.gov.au lists no entry requirements for the skill set. A Unique Student Identifier, photo identification, and an LLND check (or a Certificate III or higher) are still required, and places are for domestic students. Eligibility is limited to Australian citizens, permanent residents, and New Zealand citizens who live in Australia. People overseas or on a student visa cannot enrol, and the offering is not CRICOS-registered.

The official unit list is on the Contact Centre Team Manager Skill Set page at 3CIR Online.

A Statement of Attainment for BSBSS00127 is only as useful as the unit list it records. For the Contact Centre Team Manager Skill Set, that list is the cluster around difficult conversations, service strategies, team effectiveness and a customer-centred culture. The Contact Centre Team Manager Skill Set keeps the unit titles, the statement-of-attainment wording, and the enrolment conditions in one place, which is the safest way to check the scope before you apply.
