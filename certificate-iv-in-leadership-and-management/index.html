A practical guide to the Certificate IV in Leadership and Management (BSB40520)

BSB40520 — the Certificate IV in Leadership and Management — is a nationally recognised AQF Level 4 qualification. On the Certificate IV in Leadership and Management course page, 3CIR Online sets out a 100% online, self-paced pathway. Students complete 12 units: 5 core and 7 elective. The published unit list includes leadership in the workplace, effective workplace relationships, coordinating business operational plans, workplace communication strategies, leading and facilitating a team and critical thinking applied to work practices. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualification.

The published full price is $2,500, GST-free. There is no formal prior qualification required to enrol. Students need intermediate English language, literacy, numeracy and digital skills, to be 18 or older, Australian residency and reliable internet access. Study sits inside a 18-month enrolment window, and the AQF Volume of Learning listed on the course page is 600–2,400 hours. Assessment is practical rather than exams, and an allocated trainer marks submissions. Roles listed on the page include Team Leader, Frontline Manager, Operations Coordinator, Office Manager, Project Coordinator and Service Manager.

People who already use these skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning rather than studying the units as a course. 3CIR offers Recognition of Prior Learning for Certificate IV in Leadership and Management at $1,150, fixed, all-inclusive and GST-free. The first assessment is free and without obligation. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course, and the RPL price is the full fixed price.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. Current course details, unit lists and enrolment steps are on the course page.
