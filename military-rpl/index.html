Recognition of prior learning for current and former ADF members

Military service in the Australian Defence Force can produce a substantial evidence base: postings, trade work, leadership, safety, logistics, security and investigations among them. Recognition of prior learning asks whether that evidence meets the units in a chosen nationally recognised qualification.

3CIR's public page on the subject is military recognition of prior learning with 3CIR. It is written for current and former members who want a clear outline before they speak with the team.

The organisation is based in Brisbane and works Australia-wide. This article is an introduction only; the live military RPL page holds the published detail.
