Leaving the ADF: a public guide to recognition of prior learning

Serving and former members of the Australian Defence Force often hold skills that a civilian qualification is designed to describe. Recognition of prior learning is the assessment used to test that match, based on evidence from service rather than on starting a taught course from scratch.

3CIR's public transition guide is 3CIR's guide for serving and former ADF members. It explains how the pathway is usually organised and which qualification fields are commonly discussed, including leadership and management, work health and safety, project management, security and risk, government investigations and business.

The guide does not promise a particular qualification or a particular result. It is a practical outline for people in transition, and the live page should be read before any personal enquiry.
