Statement of Attainment notes for BSBSS00119

The Customer Service Skill Set (BSBSS00119) is a nationally recognised skill set of four units. 3CIR Online students who complete it receive a Statement of Attainment, not a full qualification. That is the outcome described on the published BSBSS00119 page.

The statement records these units: Deliver and monitor a service to customers; Process customer complaints; Assist with customer difficulties; and Advise on products and services. They form a compact customer-service cluster. They do not, on their own, make up a business certificate or diploma.

The program is online and self-paced, with no exams. Asset College issues the Statement of Attainment under the third-party arrangement set out at the end of this guide.

On enrolment, the course page sets out a short list of conditions. training.gov.au lists no entry requirements for the skill set. A Unique Student Identifier, photo identification, and an LLND check (or a Certificate III or higher) are still required, and places are for domestic students. Eligibility is limited to Australian citizens, permanent residents, and New Zealand citizens who live in Australia. People overseas or on a student visa cannot enrol, and the offering is not CRICOS-registered.

See the unit titles on the Customer Service Skill Set page at 3CIR Online before you decide.

A Statement of Attainment for BSBSS00119 is only as useful as the unit list it records. For the Customer Service Skill Set, that list is the cluster around service delivery, complaints, customer difficulties and product or service advice. The Customer Service Skill Set keeps the unit titles, the statement-of-attainment wording, and the enrolment conditions in one place, which is the safest way to check the scope before you apply.
