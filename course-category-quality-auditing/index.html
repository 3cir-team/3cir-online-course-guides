A guide to Online Quality Auditing courses with 3CIR Online

One nationally recognised Diploma is listed on the Online Quality Auditing courses page. It sits at AQF Level 5. The list is the BSB50920 Diploma of Quality Auditing. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualifications. 3CIR Online delivers the courses 100% online and self-paced.

The category page is built around this single credential. It describes the Diploma as the qualification that maps to the ISO 19011 audit standard and as the formal credential between quality-assurance experience and lead-auditor certification pathways. The page says the qualification is used where employers ask for a diploma in quality or auditing, or for someone who can plan, conduct and report on ISO audits, across manufacturing, healthcare, finance, construction, food and government. Students complete 12 units: four core and eight elective. Study is 100% online and self-paced, inside an 18-month enrolment window, with portfolio-style practical assessment rather than exams and an allocated trainer. The published unit list on the course page includes initiating quality audits, leading audit teams, reporting on audits, and related quality and compliance work.

The page points to destinations such as Quality Officer or QA Coordinator, Quality Inspector, Quality Analyst, Internal Auditor or Quality Auditor, Quality Manager and Head of Quality or Compliance Director. Use the category page for the overview, then open the Diploma course page for the full unit list and entry information before you enrol.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. People who already use the relevant skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning instead of studying the units as a course. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course. Current unit lists, entry rules and enrolment steps are on each individual course page, and on the category page itself.
