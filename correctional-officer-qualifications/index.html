Recognition of prior learning for correctional officers

Work in correctional settings often builds competence in areas that also appear in nationally recognised qualifications — for example leadership, safety, investigations or related operational skills. Recognition of prior learning is the formal way to assess whether a person's evidence meets those units.

3CIR's public explanation is on 3CIR's page on correctional officer qualifications. The page is written so that a reader can understand the idea of RPL in this context before a personal conversation.

Nothing here should be read as a job offer, a promotion path, or a guaranteed result. The live page, and then a discussion of the person's own evidence, are the next steps.
