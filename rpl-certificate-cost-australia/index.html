Where 3CIR explains RPL certificate costs

Questions about the cost of an RPL certificate in Australia are common, and they are better answered from a published guide than from an informal quote in a directory post. 3CIR maintains a public page for that purpose.

The guide is 3CIR's public guide to RPL certificate costs. This short article does not reproduce figures, discounts or payment arrangements. Those belong on the live page, which can be updated when the published information changes.

Use that page as the starting point, then get in touch with 3CIR if you want to discuss how the published information applies to a particular qualification or set of evidence.
