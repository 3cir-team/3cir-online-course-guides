A guide to Online Security and Risk Management courses with 3CIR Online

Four nationally recognised security and risk qualifications are listed on the Online Security and Risk Management courses page. They sit at Certificate IV and Diploma. The list is the 22603VIC Certificate IV in Cyber Security, the CPP40719 Certificate IV in Security Management, the CPP41519 Certificate IV in Security Risk Analysis, and the CPP50619 Diploma of Security Risk Management. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualifications. 3CIR Online delivers the courses 100% online and self-paced.

The category page groups these as pathways for people who protect people, data and assets. The Certificate IV in Cyber Security focuses on technical cyber work such as testing, identity and access management, threat detection and incident response. The Certificate IV in Security Management is described around supervising security operations, assessing client security needs, governance and leading a team. The Certificate IV in Security Risk Analysis focuses on assessing threats and vulnerabilities, advising on client security needs and developing security risk management plans, including work on crowded-place security. The Diploma of Security Risk Management sits at AQF Level 5 and covers assessing options, developing and implementing security risk management plans, and analysing the threat environment. Entry to that Diploma requires one of CPP40707, CPP40719 or CPP41519, plus intermediate language, literacy, numeracy and digital skills, Australian residency and reliable internet access.

The page points to destinations such as Security Officer or Site Supervisor, Cyber Security Analyst, Security or Risk Consultant, Security Manager, Risk Manager and Head of Security. All four qualifications are delivered 100% online and self-paced, with practical assessment and an allocated trainer. Choose the course page that matches the work you do — cyber, operational security, risk analysis, or management of security risk.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. People who already use the relevant skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning instead of studying the units as a course. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course. Current unit lists, entry rules and enrolment steps are on each individual course page, and on the category page itself.
