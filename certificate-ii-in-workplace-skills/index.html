A practical guide to the Certificate II in Workplace Skills (BSB20120)

BSB20120 — the Certificate II in Workplace Skills — is a nationally recognised AQF Level 2 qualification. On the Certificate II in Workplace Skills course page, 3CIR Online sets out a 100% online, self-paced pathway. Students complete 10 units: 5 core and 5 elective. The published unit list includes workplace communication and professional writing, digital technology and business software, customer service and engagement, workplace health and safety contribution, personal time management and sustainable work practices. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualification.

The published full price is $1,000, GST-free. There are no formal academic prerequisites. Students need intermediate language, literacy and numeracy, a Unique Student Identifier and photo identification. Study sits inside a 18-month enrolment window, and the AQF Volume of Learning listed on the course page is 600–1,200 hours. Assessment is practical rather than exams, and an allocated trainer marks submissions. Roles listed on the page include Office Administrator, Customer Service Representative, Receptionist, Data Entry Operator, Retail Sales Assistant and Call Centre Operator.

People who already use these skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning rather than studying the units as a course. 3CIR offers Recognition of Prior Learning for Certificate II in Workplace Skills at $495, fixed, all-inclusive and GST-free. The first assessment is free and without obligation. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course, and the RPL price is the full fixed price.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. Current course details, unit lists and enrolment steps are on the course page.
