Recognition of prior learning for people in Adelaide

People living in Adelaide, or elsewhere in South Australia, can have work, military or emergency services experience assessed toward a nationally recognised qualification without needing a local branch office. 3CIR is based in Bulimba, Queensland, and offers the same service Australia-wide.

The dedicated state page, recognition of prior learning in Adelaide, explains how that pathway is described for South Australian clients. Fields commonly discussed include leadership and management, work health and safety, project management, security and risk, government investigations and business, at levels from Certificate II to Graduate Diploma depending on the evidence.

This guide does not invent a South Australian street address, and it does not promise a particular result. It exists to point readers to the live Adelaide page and to the contact details at the end.
