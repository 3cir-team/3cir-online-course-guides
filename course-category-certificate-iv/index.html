A guide to Certificate IV courses online with 3CIR Online

Thirteen nationally recognised Certificate IV qualifications across six disciplines are listed on the nationally recognised certificate IV courses online page. They sit at AQF Level 4. The list is Business (BSB40120 and BSB40320), Leadership and Management (BSB40520), Project Management Practice (BSB40920), Work Health and Safety (BSB41419), Cyber Security (22603VIC), Security Management (CPP40719), Security Risk Analysis (CPP41519), Government Security including the Personnel Vetting and Fraud Control streams (PSP40316), and Government Investigations including the Regulatory Compliance stream (PSP40416). Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualifications. 3CIR Online delivers the courses 100% online and self-paced.

The category page groups these as specialist Certificate IV qualifications for people stepping up from doing the work to coordinating or advising on it. You can filter by discipline — business, leadership and management, project management, work health and safety, security and risk, or government — and then open the individual course page for the unit list and any elective rules. Delivery is 100% online and self-paced, with practical assessment rather than exams and an allocated trainer. Most of these qualifications sit inside an 18-month enrolment window. Several government and security qualifications share a national code but are listed as distinct streams, so it is worth reading the specific course page before enrolling.

The page points to destinations such as Business Development Coordinator, Frontline Manager, WHS Officer or Coordinator, Project Coordinator, Security Risk Analyst and Government Vetting Officer. The page places Certificate IV between Certificate III and Diploma on the pathway: a specialist credential in its own right, and a common step toward a Diploma in the same field.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. People who already use the relevant skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning instead of studying the units as a course. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course. Current unit lists, entry rules and enrolment steps are on each individual course page, and on the category page itself.
