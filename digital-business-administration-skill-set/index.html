What BSBSS00113 puts on a Statement of Attainment

A Statement of Attainment for the Digital Business Administration Skill Set records the skill-set code BSBSS00113 and four units, not a full qualification title. 3CIR Online delivers the skill set; Asset College issues the statement. The published BSBSS00113 page is clear that this is not a complete qualification.

The four units are Design and produce complex text documents; Design and produce complex spreadsheets; Apply digital solutions to work processes; and Use digital technologies to collaborate in a work environment. That is the entire scope of the award, aimed at business administration roles that use these digital skills.

Study is online and self-paced, and there are no exams. The issuing registered training organisation is the one named on the course page: Asset College.

On enrolment, the course page sets out a short list of conditions. training.gov.au lists no entry requirements for the skill set. A Unique Student Identifier, photo identification, and an LLND check (or a Certificate III or higher) are still required, and places are for domestic students. Eligibility is limited to Australian citizens, permanent residents, and New Zealand citizens who live in Australia. People overseas or on a student visa cannot enrol, and the offering is not CRICOS-registered.

The unit list is on the Digital Business Administration Skill Set page at 3CIR Online.

A Statement of Attainment for BSBSS00113 is only as useful as the unit list it records. For the Digital Business Administration Skill Set, that list is the cluster around complex documents and spreadsheets, digital solutions and online collaboration. The Digital Business Administration Skill Set keeps the unit titles, the statement-of-attainment wording, and the enrolment conditions in one place, which is the safest way to check the scope before you apply.
