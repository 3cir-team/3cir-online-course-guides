Statement of Attainment notes for BSBSS00100

The Business Operations Support Skill Set does not confer a full qualification. It is a nationally recognised skill set, code BSBSS00100, made up of five units. 3CIR Online students who complete it receive a Statement of Attainment that lists BSBSS00100 and those units. The point is made on the published BSBSS00100 page.

The five units are Research and develop business plans; Report on financial activity; Coordinate business resources; Coordinate business operational plans; and Apply business risk management processes. That operations-support cluster is the entire scope of the statement. It is not a diploma or certificate in business, even though some of the unit titles also appear in longer qualifications.

Delivery is online and self-paced with 3CIR Online, with no exams. Asset College issues the Statement of Attainment under the third-party arrangement described at the end of this guide.

On enrolment, the course page sets out a short list of conditions. training.gov.au lists no entry requirements for the skill set. A Unique Student Identifier, photo identification, and an LLND check (or a Certificate III or higher) are still required, and places are for domestic students. Eligibility is limited to Australian citizens, permanent residents, and New Zealand citizens who live in Australia. People overseas or on a student visa cannot enrol, and the offering is not CRICOS-registered.

The five unit titles and the enrolment conditions are on the Business Operations Support Skill Set page at 3CIR Online.

A Statement of Attainment for BSBSS00100 is only as useful as the unit list it records. For the Business Operations Support Skill Set, that list is the cluster around business plans, financial reporting, resources, operational plans and risk. The Business Operations Support Skill Set keeps the unit titles, the statement-of-attainment wording, and the enrolment conditions in one place, which is the safest way to check the scope before you apply.
