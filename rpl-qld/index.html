Queensland recognition of prior learning with 3CIR

Because 3CIR is based in Bulimba, Brisbane, a large share of its recognition of prior learning work is with people who live in Queensland — in the capital and in regional centres. The same team also works Australia-wide.

The state page, Queensland recognition of prior learning with 3CIR, describes how existing experience can be assessed toward a nationally recognised qualification. Common fields include leadership and management, work health and safety, project management, security and risk, government investigations and business, at levels from Certificate II to Graduate Diploma.

This short guide exists to point Queensland readers to that live page. It does not replace a conversation about a person's own evidence, and it does not promise a particular outcome.
