Statement of Attainment notes for BSBSS00103

The New Business Ventures Skill Set is a nationally recognised skill set, code BSBSS00103, of four units. Completing it through 3CIR Online leads to a Statement of Attainment, not a full qualification. The published BSBSS00103 page states that clearly.

The statement records Investigate business opportunities; Develop and present business proposals; Organise finances for new business ventures; and Determine resource requirements for new business ventures. Compared with the two-unit Micro Business Skill Set, this award adds a proposal, start-up finances and a resource plan. It still is not a complete entrepreneurship qualification.

Delivery is online and self-paced, with no exams. Asset College issues the Statement of Attainment under the third-party arrangement at the end of this guide.

On enrolment, the course page sets out a short list of conditions. training.gov.au lists no entry requirements for the skill set. A Unique Student Identifier, photo identification, and an LLND check (or a Certificate III or higher) are still required, and places are for domestic students. Eligibility is limited to Australian citizens, permanent residents, and New Zealand citizens who live in Australia. People overseas or on a student visa cannot enrol, and the offering is not CRICOS-registered.

See the four unit titles on the New Business Ventures Skill Set page at 3CIR Online.

A Statement of Attainment for BSBSS00103 is only as useful as the unit list it records. For the New Business Ventures Skill Set, that list is the cluster around investigating an opportunity, presenting a proposal, organising start-up finances and planning resources. The New Business Ventures Skill Set keeps the unit titles, the statement-of-attainment wording, and the enrolment conditions in one place, which is the safest way to check the scope before you apply.
