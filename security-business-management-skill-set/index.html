What CPPSS00078 records on a Statement of Attainment

The Security Business Management Skill Set (CPPSS00078) is a nationally recognised skill set of five units, not a full security or business qualification. 3CIR Online students who complete it receive a Statement of Attainment listing CPPSS00078 and those units. The published CPPSS00078 page states that it is not a full qualification.

The five units are Establish legal and risk management requirements of new business ventures; Coordinate recruitment and onboarding; Coordinate business resources; Develop personal work priorities; and Implement contracting arrangements for security businesses. The last title is specific to security contracting, which is why this CPP skill set should not be read as a general business-operations award.

Study is online and self-paced, with no exams. Asset College is the issuing registered training organisation named on the page.

On enrolment, the course page sets out a short list of conditions. training.gov.au lists no entry requirements for the skill set. A Unique Student Identifier, photo identification, and an LLND check (or a Certificate III or higher) are still required, and places are for domestic students. Eligibility is limited to Australian citizens, permanent residents, and New Zealand citizens who live in Australia. People overseas or on a student visa cannot enrol, and the offering is not CRICOS-registered.

The five unit titles are on the Security Business Management Skill Set page at 3CIR Online.

A Statement of Attainment for CPPSS00078 is only as useful as the unit list it records. For the Security Business Management Skill Set, that list is the cluster around legal and risk duties, recruitment, resources, personal work priorities and security contracting. The Security Business Management Skill Set keeps the unit titles, the statement-of-attainment wording, and the enrolment conditions in one place, which is the safest way to check the scope before you apply.
