A guide to Online Government qualifications with 3CIR Online

Seven nationally recognised government qualifications are listed on the Online Government qualifications page. They sit at Certificate IV and Diploma. The list is the PSP40316 Certificate IV in Government Security, plus the Personnel Vetting and Fraud Control streams of the same code; the PSP40416 Certificate IV in Government Investigations, plus the Regulatory Compliance stream; the PSP50316 Diploma of Government Security; and the PSP50416 Diploma of Government Investigations. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualifications. 3CIR Online delivers the courses 100% online and self-paced.

The category page presents these as specialist public-sector pathways rather than a single generic government course. The security streams cover handling sensitive information, assessing and treating risk, security awareness and, in the named streams, personnel security assessments or fraud-control work. The investigations streams cover planning, conducting and finalising an investigation, applying regulatory powers, interviews, official notes and briefs of evidence, with the Regulatory Compliance stream placing more weight on compliance audits and acting on non-compliance. The two Diplomas sit at AQF Level 5 and focus on coordinating investigations or protective security at a more senior level, including threat-environment analysis and security risk management planning. All seven are delivered 100% online and self-paced, with practical assessment and an allocated trainer, typically inside an 18-month enrolment window.

The page points to destinations such as government security adviser, personnel vetting officer, fraud control officer, government investigator, compliance officer and investigations or security manager, depending on the stream. Because several listings share a national code, read the specific stream page so the electives and occupational focus match the work you do or want to do.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. People who already use the relevant skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning instead of studying the units as a course. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course. Current unit lists, entry rules and enrolment steps are on each individual course page, and on the category page itself.
