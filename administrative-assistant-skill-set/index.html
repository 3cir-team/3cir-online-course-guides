What a Statement of Attainment means for BSBSS00120

A skill set is not a certificate or a diploma. The Administrative Assistant Skill Set (BSBSS00120) is a nationally recognised group of four units. When those units are completed through 3CIR Online, the outcome is a Statement of Attainment, which records BSBSS00120 and the units achieved, not a full qualification title. The distinction is stated on the published BSBSS00120 page.

The four units — Maintain financial records; Maintain business resources; Organise schedules; and Purchase goods and services — are the whole of the award. People often look at this skill set when they assist with administrative tasks and need that cluster recognised, without enrolling in a longer business qualification.

3CIR Online hosts the learning online and self-paced, and there are no exams. The issuing body for the Statement of Attainment is Asset College, the registered training organisation named on the course page.

On enrolment, the course page sets out a short list of conditions. training.gov.au lists no entry requirements for the skill set. A Unique Student Identifier, photo identification, and an LLND check (or a Certificate III or higher) are still required, and places are for domestic students. Eligibility is limited to Australian citizens, permanent residents, and New Zealand citizens who live in Australia. People overseas or on a student visa cannot enrol, and the offering is not CRICOS-registered.

Read the unit list on the Administrative Assistant Skill Set page at 3CIR Online before you decide whether this short award is the right fit.

A Statement of Attainment for BSBSS00120 is only as useful as the unit list it records. For the Administrative Assistant Skill Set, that list is the cluster around financial records, business resources, diaries and purchasing for people who assist with administrative tasks. The Administrative Assistant Skill Set keeps the unit titles, the statement-of-attainment wording, and the enrolment conditions in one place, which is the safest way to check the scope before you apply.
