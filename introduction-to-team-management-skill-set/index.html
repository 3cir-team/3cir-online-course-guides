Statement of Attainment notes for BSBSS00109

The Introduction to Team Management Skill Set (BSBSS00109) is a short, nationally recognised award, not a Certificate IV or Diploma of Leadership and Management. 3CIR Online students who complete the four units receive a Statement of Attainment listing BSBSS00109 and those units. The published BSBSS00109 page makes that distinction.

The four units are Communicate with influence; Manage people performance; Manage project human resources; and Lead and facilitate a team. They are written for people who are starting to lead and manage a team. The statement does not confer a full leadership qualification.

The program is online and self-paced, with no exams. Asset College is the issuing registered training organisation named on the page.

On enrolment, the course page sets out a short list of conditions. training.gov.au lists no entry requirements for the skill set. A Unique Student Identifier, photo identification, and an LLND check (or a Certificate III or higher) are still required, and places are for domestic students. Eligibility is limited to Australian citizens, permanent residents, and New Zealand citizens who live in Australia. People overseas or on a student visa cannot enrol, and the offering is not CRICOS-registered.

See the unit list on the Introduction to Team Management Skill Set page at 3CIR Online.

A Statement of Attainment for BSBSS00109 is only as useful as the unit list it records. For the Introduction to Team Management Skill Set, that list is the cluster around influential communication, people performance, project staffing and leading a team. The Introduction to Team Management Skill Set keeps the unit titles, the statement-of-attainment wording, and the enrolment conditions in one place, which is the safest way to check the scope before you apply.
