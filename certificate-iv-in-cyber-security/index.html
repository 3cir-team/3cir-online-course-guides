A practical guide to the Certificate IV in Cyber Security (22603VIC)

22603VIC — the Certificate IV in Cyber Security — is a nationally recognised AQF Level 4 qualification. On the Certificate IV in Cyber Security course page, 3CIR Online sets out a 100% online, self-paced pathway. Students complete 16 units: 8 core and 8 elective. The published unit list includes network concepts and protocols required in cyber security, cyber security legislation, privacy and ethical practices, cyber security testing concepts and procedures, security infrastructure management, identity and access management and cyber security data analysis and threat detection. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualification.

The published full price is $2,500, GST-free. There is no formal prior qualification required to enrol. Students need intermediate English language, literacy, numeracy and digital skills, to be 18 or older, Australian residency and reliable internet access. The course is delivered 100% online and is self-paced, and the AQF Volume of Learning listed on the course page is 600–2,400 hours. Assessment is practical rather than exams, and an allocated trainer marks submissions. Roles listed on the page include IT Security Officer, Cyber Security Analyst, SOC Analyst, Information Security Analyst, Cyber Security Consultant and Cyber Security Engineer.

People who already use these skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning rather than studying the units as a course. 3CIR offers Recognition of Prior Learning for Certificate IV in Cyber Security at $1,150, fixed, all-inclusive and GST-free. The first assessment is free and without obligation. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course, and the RPL price is the full fixed price.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. Current course details, unit lists and enrolment steps are on the course page.
