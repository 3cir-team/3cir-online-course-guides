A guide to Online Cyber Security courses with 3CIR Online

One nationally recognised cyber security qualification is listed on the Online Cyber Security courses page. It sits at AQF Level 4. The list is the 22603VIC Certificate IV in Cyber Security. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualifications. 3CIR Online delivers the courses 100% online and self-paced.

The category page is dedicated to this single qualification, which 3CIR Online delivers 100% online and self-paced. Students complete 16 units: eight core and eight elective. The published unit list includes network concepts and protocols required in cyber security, cyber security legislation, privacy and ethical practices, testing concepts and procedures, security infrastructure management, identity and access management, data analysis and threat detection, incident response, web vulnerability assessment, system security monitoring, disaster recovery and an industry project. The course page records that 22603VIC is a state-accredited course, accredited by the Victorian Registration and Qualifications Authority for the period 1 January 2023 to 31 December 2027, and is nationally recognised. There is no formal prior qualification required to enrol. Students need intermediate English language, literacy, numeracy and digital skills, to be 18 or older, Australian residency and reliable internet access.

The page points to destinations such as IT Security Officer, Cyber Security Analyst, SOC Analyst, Information Security Analyst, Cyber Security Consultant and Cyber Security Engineer. Assessment is practical rather than exams, and an allocated trainer marks submissions. The AQF Volume of Learning listed on the course page is 600–2,400 hours.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. People who already use the relevant skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning instead of studying the units as a course. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course. Current unit lists, entry rules and enrolment steps are on each individual course page, and on the category page itself.
