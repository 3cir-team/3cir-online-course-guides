A practical guide to the Diploma of Work Health and Safety (BSB51319)

BSB51319 — the Diploma of Work Health and Safety — is a nationally recognised AQF Level 5 qualification. On the Diploma of Work Health and Safety course page, 3CIR Online sets out a 100% online, self-paced pathway. Students complete 10 units: 5 core and 5 elective. The published unit list includes leading WHS risk management, leading initial response to and investigating WHS incidents, developing, implementing and maintaining a WHS management system, leading the development and use of WHS risk management tools, managing WHS consultation and participation processes and managing a WHS information system. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualification.

The published full price is $3,350, GST-free. Entry requires completion of the core units of BSB41419 Certificate IV in Work Health and Safety, or mapped-equivalent predecessor competencies, plus intermediate language, literacy, numeracy and digital skills, Australian residency and reliable internet access. Study sits inside a 18-month enrolment window, and the AQF Volume of Learning listed on the course page is 1,200–2,400 hours. Assessment is practical rather than exams, and an allocated trainer marks submissions. Roles listed on the page include WHS Manager, Health & Safety Manager, HSE Advisor, Health & Safety Adviser, Return to Work Coordinator and WHS Coordinator.

People who already use these skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning rather than studying the units as a course. 3CIR offers Recognition of Prior Learning for Diploma of Work Health and Safety at $1,500, fixed, all-inclusive and GST-free. The first assessment is free and without obligation. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course, and the RPL price is the full fixed price.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. Current course details, unit lists and enrolment steps are on the course page.
