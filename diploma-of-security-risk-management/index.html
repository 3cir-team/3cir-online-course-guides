A practical guide to the Diploma of Security Risk Management (CPP50619)

CPP50619 — the Diploma of Security Risk Management — is a nationally recognised AQF Level 5 qualification. On the Diploma of Security Risk Management course page, 3CIR Online sets out a 100% online, self-paced pathway. Students complete 10 units: 5 core and 5 elective. The published unit list includes assessing security risk management options, developing security risk management plans, implementing security risk management plans, analysing the threat environment and devising active-threat response strategies, implementing communication systems to respond to active threats and coordinating security operations. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualification.

The published full price is $3,350, GST-free. Entry requires one of the Certificate IV qualifications in Security and Risk Management (CPP40707), Security Management (CPP40719), or Security Risk Analysis (CPP41519), plus intermediate language, literacy, numeracy and digital skills, Australian residency and reliable internet access. Study sits inside a 18-month enrolment window, and the AQF Volume of Learning listed on the course page is 1,200–2,400 hours. Assessment is practical rather than exams, and an allocated trainer marks submissions. Roles listed on the page include Risk Manager, Security Manager, Security Consultant, Risk & Compliance Manager, Security Specialist and Security Risk Adviser.

People who already use these skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning rather than studying the units as a course. 3CIR offers Recognition of Prior Learning for Diploma of Security Risk Management at $1,500, fixed, all-inclusive and GST-free. The first assessment is free and without obligation. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course, and the RPL price is the full fixed price.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. Current course details, unit lists and enrolment steps are on the course page.
