Recognition of prior learning for people in Melbourne

Melbourne and the rest of Victoria are part of the area 3CIR already serves. Because the organisation works Australia-wide from its Brisbane office, a person in Victoria can use the same recognition of prior learning pathway as a client in Queensland or New South Wales.

Start with RPL for people in Melbourne. That page describes how existing experience can be assessed against nationally recognised qualifications, and which fields are commonly involved — leadership and management, work health and safety, project management, security and risk, government investigations and business.

This note is an introduction only. It does not replace the live Melbourne page, and it does not treat a qualification as automatic. The assessment depends on the evidence presented against the chosen qualification.
