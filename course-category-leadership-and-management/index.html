A guide to Leadership and Management courses online with 3CIR Online

Three nationally recognised leadership and management qualifications are listed on the nationally recognised leadership and Management courses online page. They sit at Certificate IV, Diploma and Advanced Diploma. The list is the BSB40520 Certificate IV in Leadership and Management, the BSB50420 Diploma of Leadership and Management, and the BSB60420 Advanced Diploma of Leadership and Management. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualifications. 3CIR Online delivers the courses 100% online and self-paced.

The category page is organised as a clear three-level pathway. The Certificate IV is described as being for new and emerging leaders: leading a team, workplace relationships, communication strategies and operational plans. Students complete 12 units (five core and seven elective) inside an 18-month enrolment window. The Diploma is described as being for managers who plan and monitor the work of others, covering influence, critical thinking, emotional intelligence and team effectiveness, in 12 units (six core and six elective). The Advanced Diploma is described as being for senior leaders, covering organisational change, strategic planning, leadership development and operational planning, in 10 units (five core and five elective) inside a 24-month enrolment term. Entry to the Advanced Diploma is limited to people who have completed a Diploma or Advanced Diploma from the BSB Training Package, or who have two years of equivalent full-time relevant workplace experience in an operational or leadership role.

The page points to destinations such as team leader and frontline supervisor roles at Certificate IV, operational and departmental manager roles at Diploma, and senior leadership roles at Advanced Diploma. All three qualifications are 100% online and self-paced, with practical assessment rather than exams and an allocated trainer. Choose the level that matches the responsibility you already have or are moving into.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. People who already use the relevant skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning instead of studying the units as a course. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course. Current unit lists, entry rules and enrolment steps are on each individual course page, and on the category page itself.
