A walk-through of 3CIR's step-by-step RPL guide

The recognition of prior learning process is easier to understand when it is broken into ordinary steps: an initial conversation, a look at the chosen qualification, a plan for evidence, then formal assessment against the units. 3CIR keeps a public guide that describes that sequence.

Read a step-by-step guide to the RPL process for the current walk-through. The guide is written so a person can see how the work is usually organised before they decide whether to start.

Evidence often includes workplace documents, third-party reports and interviews. The qualification, if the evidence supports one, may range from Certificate II to Graduate Diploma. This page is only a pointer to that live guide.
