A practical guide to the Diploma of Project Management (BSB50820)

BSB50820 — the Diploma of Project Management — is a nationally recognised AQF Level 5 qualification. On the Diploma of Project Management course page, 3CIR Online sets out a 100% online, self-paced pathway. Students complete 12 units: 8 core and 4 elective. The published unit list includes manage project scope, manage project time and schedules, manage project quality, manage project cost and budgets, manage project human resources and manage project information and communication. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualification.

The published full price is $3,350, GST-free. There is no formal prior qualification required to enrol. Students need intermediate English language, literacy, numeracy and digital skills, to be 18 or older, Australian residency and reliable internet access. Study sits inside a 18-month enrolment window, and the AQF Volume of Learning listed on the course page is 1,200–2,400 hours. Assessment is practical rather than exams, and an allocated trainer marks submissions. Roles listed on the page include Project Manager, Project Scheduler, IT Project Manager, Program Coordinator, Project Management Officer and Project Coordinator.

People who already use these skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning rather than studying the units as a course. 3CIR offers Recognition of Prior Learning for Diploma of Project Management at $1,500, fixed, all-inclusive and GST-free. The first assessment is free and without obligation. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course, and the RPL price is the full fixed price.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. Current course details, unit lists and enrolment steps are on the course page.
