A guide to Certificate II courses online with 3CIR Online

One nationally recognised Certificate II qualification is listed on the nationally recognised certificate II courses online page. It sits at AQF Level 2. The list is the BSB20120 Certificate II in Workplace Skills. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualifications. 3CIR Online delivers the courses 100% online and self-paced.

The category page describes this as the foundation credential for people starting their working life or returning after time away, with no formal academic prerequisites. Students need intermediate language, literacy and numeracy, a Unique Student Identifier and photo identification. The course is 10 units (five core and five elective) and sits inside an 18-month enrolment window. The units cover workplace communication and professional writing, digital technology and business software, customer service, contributing to workplace health and safety, time management, sustainable work practices, data entry, financial transaction processing, problem-solving and working with others. Assessment is practical rather than exams, and an allocated trainer marks submissions.

The page points to destinations such as Office Administrator, Receptionist, Customer Service Officer, Data Entry Operator, Administrative Assistant and Sales Support Officer. The page also signposts the next step to Certificate III qualifications and to the wider 3CIR Online catalogue once the foundation units are complete.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. People who already use the relevant skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning instead of studying the units as a course. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course. Current unit lists, entry rules and enrolment steps are on each individual course page, and on the category page itself.
