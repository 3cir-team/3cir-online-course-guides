A guide to Certificate III courses online with 3CIR Online

Three nationally recognised Certificate III qualifications are listed on the nationally recognised certificate III courses online page. They sit at AQF Level 3. The list is the BSB30120 Certificate III in Business, the BSB30220 Certificate III in Entrepreneurship and New Business, and the BSB30719 Certificate III in Work Health and Safety. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualifications. 3CIR Online delivers the courses 100% online and self-paced.

The landing page asks you to pick the qualification that matches where you want to go. The Certificate III in Business is presented as a step into supervisor, senior administrator and team-leader work, with skills in operations, customer service and workplace coordination. The Certificate III in Entrepreneurship and New Business is described as helping someone plan, finance and operate a small business, including tradespeople, freelancers and people formalising a side venture. The Certificate III in Work Health and Safety is described as the credential behind site safety officer, WHS representative and health and safety coordinator roles. All three are delivered fully online and self-paced, with an allocated trainer marking practical assessments rather than exams. Study sits inside an 18-month enrolment window.

The page points to destinations such as Office Supervisor, Team Leader, Operations Coordinator, Small Business Owner, Site Safety Officer and WHS Coordinator. The page places Certificate III between Certificate II and Certificate IV on the vocational pathway, so it can be a first recognised qualification or a stepping stone to a specialist Certificate IV.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. People who already use the relevant skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning instead of studying the units as a course. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course. Current unit lists, entry rules and enrolment steps are on each individual course page, and on the category page itself.
