A guide to Project Management courses online with 3CIR Online

Two nationally recognised project management qualifications are listed on the nationally recognised project Management courses online page. They sit at Certificate IV and Diploma. The list is the BSB40920 Certificate IV in Project Management Practice and the BSB50820 Diploma of Project Management. Asset College (RTO 31718) issues the qualifications. 3CIR Online delivers the courses 100% online and self-paced.

The category page describes two levels rather than a long menu of options. The Certificate IV is for people who support projects as part of a team. It covers scope, time and quality techniques, with electives that can include cost, risk and stakeholder engagement. Students complete nine units (three core and six elective). The Diploma is for people who lead projects. It covers scope, time, quality, cost, human resources, communication, risk and integration management, in 12 units (eight core and four elective). The category page states that neither of these two online courses has formal entry requirements. Both are delivered 100% online and self-paced, typically inside an 18-month enrolment window, with practical assessment rather than exams and an allocated trainer.

The page points to destinations such as project coordinator, project officer and project team roles after the Certificate IV, and project manager roles after the Diploma. The page also mentions Recognition of Prior Learning for people who already do this work, and points to higher-level RPL pathways. Those options are assessments of existing evidence, not online courses on this category page, and should be read on their own pages.

3CIR can enrol Australian citizens, Australian permanent residents and New Zealand citizens living in Australia. 3CIR is not CRICOS-registered and cannot enrol people based overseas or on a student visa. People who already use the relevant skills at work may prefer Recognition of Prior Learning instead of studying the units as a course. RPL is an assessment of evidence you already have, including paid work, self-employment or volunteering where you can show the skill. It is not a course. Current unit lists, entry rules and enrolment steps are on each individual course page, and on the category page itself.
