Recognition of prior learning for people in Sydney

Sydney and the rest of New South Wales are part of 3CIR's Australia-wide service. Recognition of prior learning is offered from the Brisbane office, so a person in New South Wales does not need a local shopfront in order to have existing experience assessed.

Start with recognition of prior learning in Sydney. That page explains how work, military and emergency services backgrounds can be compared with nationally recognised qualifications, and which fields are commonly involved.

Levels under discussion may range from Certificate II to Graduate Diploma, depending on the evidence. This note is only an introduction; the Sydney page holds the current public outline.
