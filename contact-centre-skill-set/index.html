The Statement of Attainment for BSBSS00126

Completing the Contact Centre Skill Set (BSBSS00126) does not issue a full qualification. The nationally recognised outcome is a Statement of Attainment for four units, delivered online by 3CIR Online. The course page states that distinction clearly: see the published BSBSS00126 page.

The four units recorded on the statement are Collect and record data; Use digital technologies to communicate in a work environment; Assist with customer difficulties; and Advise on products and services. They describe the work of people in a customer service contact centre. The statement does not extend to a broader customer-service or business qualification.

Study is self-paced, there are no exams, and Asset College is the issuing registered training organisation named on the page.

On enrolment, the course page sets out a short list of conditions. training.gov.au lists no entry requirements for the skill set. A Unique Student Identifier, photo identification, and an LLND check (or a Certificate III or higher) are still required, and places are for domestic students. Eligibility is limited to Australian citizens, permanent residents, and New Zealand citizens who live in Australia. People overseas or on a student visa cannot enrol, and the offering is not CRICOS-registered.

Confirm the unit list on the Contact Centre Skill Set page at 3CIR Online before you enrol.

A Statement of Attainment for BSBSS00126 is only as useful as the unit list it records. For the Contact Centre Skill Set, that list is the cluster around data recording, digital communication, customer difficulties and product advice in a contact centre. The Contact Centre Skill Set keeps the unit titles, the statement-of-attainment wording, and the enrolment conditions in one place, which is the safest way to check the scope before you apply.
